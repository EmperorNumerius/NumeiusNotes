## 2024-05-14 - Accessible Icon-only Buttons via Tooltips\n**Learning:** In this Flutter app's design system, custom icon-only actions built using `GestureDetector` or `InkWell` (like the 'Close tab' or 'Note options' actions) completely lack the implicit accessible hover labels and semantics provided by the standard `IconButton`.\n**Action:** When building or auditing custom interactive elements, always explicitly wrap `InkWell` or `GestureDetector` containing only icons with a `Tooltip` widget to ensure screen reader labeling and mouse hover feedback.

## 2024-05-20 - Use IconButton over GestureDetector
**Learning:** Native `IconButton` inherently sets semantic ARIA-like labels for screen readers and provides hover states, unlike raw `GestureDetector`s or `InkWell`s.
**Action:** Use `IconButton` instead of `GestureDetector` or `InkWell` for icon-only buttons to improve accessibility and UX.
